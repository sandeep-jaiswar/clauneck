=== chemistry.redox_reactions ===
Redox chemistry: cell potentials, Nernst equation, electrochemistry, thermodynamics of half-reactions.
Quantities: standard electrode potential (V), number of electrons transferred, reaction quotient Q, temperature.
Supported operations: cell_potential, nernst_equation, gibbs_free_energy_from_cell, equilibrium_constant_from_cell.
Solver method: SYMBOLIC_SOLVE (closed-form calculations).
Example: calculate cell potential at non-standard conditions using Nernst equation.
