=== mathematics.geometry ===
Geometric calculations: areas, volumes, distances, angles.
Quantities: dimensions (lengths, radii, angles).
Supported operations: circle_area(r), sphere_volume(r), triangle_area(base, height), 
distance_2d(x1, y1, x2, y2), polygon_perimeter(side_lengths_vec).
