=== mathematics.numerical_methods ===
Root-finding, interpolation, numerical differentiation/integration, and ODE solving.
Quantities: target values, interval bounds, data points for interpolation, step sizes, tolerances.
Equations: put exactly one supported operation call in rhs, such as newton_raphson(f_code, df_code, x0),
bisection_method(f_code, a, b), lagrange_interpolation(x_data, y_data, x), or rk45_step(f_code, y, t, dt).
Supported operations: newton_raphson, bisection_method, secant_method, brent_method,
lagrange_interpolation, newton_interpolation, cubic_spline, finite_difference_1st_order,
finite_difference_2nd_order, richardson_extrapolation, trapezoidal_integration, simpsons_rule,
gauss_legendre_integration, rk45_step, bdf_step, ode_adaptive_step, solve_stiff_system.
solver.timeSpan: not required.
