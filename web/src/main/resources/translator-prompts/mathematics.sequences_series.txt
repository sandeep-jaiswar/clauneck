=== mathematics.sequences_series ===
Arithmetic, geometric, harmonic series; convergence tests; Taylor/Fourier series expansions.
Quantities: series parameters (first term, ratio/difference, number of terms, function expressions).
Equations: put exactly one supported operation call in rhs.
Supported operations: arithmetic_sum, geometric_sum, harmonic_sum, ratio_test, root_test, integral_test, alternating_series_test, power_series_convergence, taylor_series, maclaurin_series, fourier_sine_series, fourier_cosine_series, fourier_complex_series, generating_function, series_convergence_radius, nth_term.
solver.timeSpan: not required.

Operation Details:
- arithmetic_sum(a1, d, n): Sum of n terms of arithmetic sequence starting at a1 with common difference d
- geometric_sum(a, r, n): Sum of n terms of geometric sequence with first term a and ratio r
- harmonic_sum(n): Partial sum of harmonic series H_n = 1 + 1/2 + ... + 1/n
- ratio_test(a_n, a_n_plus_1): Convergence test using ratio lim|a_{n+1}/a_n|; returns limit and status
- root_test(a_n, n): Convergence test using root lim|a_n|^(1/n); returns nth-root and status
- integral_test(f_expr, a, b): Convergence via integral from a to b (use "1/n" for harmonic, "1/n**2" for p-series)
- alternating_series_test(a_n, a_n_plus_1): Check if alternating series converges (decreasing terms)
- power_series_convergence(x, radius): Check convergence of power series at point x with given radius
- taylor_series(f, var, x0, n): Taylor expansion of f around x0 with n terms; returns coefficients
- maclaurin_series(f, var, n): Maclaurin series (Taylor at x0=0) of f with n terms
- fourier_sine_series(f, L, n): Fourier sine series coefficients b_n on [0,L] with n terms
- fourier_cosine_series(f, L, n): Fourier cosine series a0 and a_n on [0,L] with n terms
- fourier_complex_series(f, L, n): Complex exponential Fourier series on [-L,L] with n terms
- generating_function(coefficients, x): Evaluate G(x) = sum(c_n * x^n) with coefficient list
- series_convergence_radius(a_n, a_n_plus_1): Estimate radius of convergence R = 1/lim|a_{n+1}/a_n|
- nth_term(a1, d_or_r, n, type): Compute nth term of arithmetic (d_or_r=d) or geometric (d_or_r=r) sequence
