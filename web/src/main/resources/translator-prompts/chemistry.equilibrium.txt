=== chemistry.equilibrium ===
Chemical equilibrium: reaction quotient Q, equilibrium constant K, Le Chatelier predictions.
Quantities: concentrations (mol/L), stoichiometric coefficients, K_eq, Q, temperature.
Supported operations: reaction_quotient, kp_from_kc, le_chatelier_direction, percent_dissociation.
Solver method: SYMBOLIC_SOLVE (closed-form calculations).
Example: calculate reaction quotient Q for aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD with current concentrations.
