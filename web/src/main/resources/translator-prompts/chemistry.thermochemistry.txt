=== chemistry.thermochemistry ===
Enthalpy, Hess's law, calorimetry, bond energies, enthalpy of reaction.
Quantities: heat (J or kJ), enthalpy change (kJ/mol), mass (g), specific heat capacity, moles.
Supported operations: hess_law_sum, standard_enthalpy_reaction, calorimetry_heat,
bond_energy_enthalpy, enthalpy_per_mole.
Solver method: SYMBOLIC_SOLVE (closed-form calculations).
Example: calculate standard enthalpy of reaction from formation enthalpies of products and reactants.
