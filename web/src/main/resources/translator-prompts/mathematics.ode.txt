=== mathematics.ode ===
Ordinary differential equation solving.
Equations: specify ODE in the form d^n/dt^n[y] = rhs(t, y, dy/dt, ...).
solver.timeSpan: required, specifies integration interval.
Supported solver methods: RK45, RK23, DOP853 for numerical integration.
