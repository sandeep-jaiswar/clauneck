=== mathematics.trigonometry ===
Trigonometric calculations: sines, cosines, tangents, inverse trig functions, angle conversions.
Quantities: angles (in deg or rad), side lengths.
Supported operations: sin(x), cos(x), tan(x), asin(x), acos(x), atan(x), deg_to_rad(angle), rad_to_deg(angle).
